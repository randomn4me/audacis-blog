Welcome to tmux!

tmux is a terminal multiplexer. It lets you switch easily between several
programs in one terminal, detach them (they keep running in the background) and
reattach them to a different terminal.

**Download [tmux 3.7a here](https://github.com/tmux/tmux/releases/download/3.7a/tmux-3.7a.tar.gz)** ([changes in this version](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/tmux/tmux/3.7a/CHANGES)).

See:

the [getting started guide](wiki/Getting-Started);
[how to install tmux](wiki/Installing);
the [tmux(1) manual page](http://man.openbsd.org/OpenBSD-current/man1/tmux.1);
and the [README](https://github.com/tmux/tmux/blob/master/.github/README.md).
For help and support:

the [FAQ](wiki/FAQ);
the [tmux-users@googlegroups.com mailing list](mailto:tmux-users@googlegroups.com).
Report bugs by [opening an issue](https://github.com/tmux/tmux/issues). Notes
on tmux development and ideas for contributions are [here](wiki/Contributing).

tmux merchandise 👕 is available [from Commit Goods](https://commitgoods.com/collections/tmux) - purchases help support the
tmux project. Or you can [donate to tmux through GitHub](https://github.com/sponsors/tmux).
