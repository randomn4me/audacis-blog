PEASEC – Science and Technology for Peace and Security

Advances in science and technology — particularly in information technology — have a profound impact on peace and security worldwide. At PEASEC (Science and Technology for Peace and Security) at [Technical University of Darmstadt](https://www.tu-darmstadt.de/), we study how digital technologies shape peace and security. Our interdisciplinary research **combines computer science with peace and security studies** within a team led by [Prof. Dr. Dr. Christian Reuter](/team/reuter). We focus on cybersecurity and privacy, human-computer interaction, and technologies in the context of peace and conflict. [[Learn more](https://peasec.de/about-us/)]
