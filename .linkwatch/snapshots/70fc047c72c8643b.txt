Firearms in the United States

The right to own [firearms in the United States](/topics/1287/firearms-in-the-us/) is enshrined in the 2nd Amendment of the U.S. Constitution, and while this right may be seen as quintessentially American, the relationship between Americans and their firearms has become fraught in the last few years. The proliferation of [mass shootings in the U.S.](/statistics/476101/worst-mass-shootings-in-the-us/) has brought the topic of gun control into the national spotlight, with support for [banning assault-style weapons](/statistics/811842/support-distribution-for-banning-assault-style-weapons-in-the-united-states/) a particularly divisive issue among Americans.
