FAQs

How long are the World Cup Specials available?

At netcup, a selection of suitable country domains compete against each other. Each time a nation is eliminated, its domain special also disappears from the tournament on the following working day. As long as the national team is still in the running at the World Cup, **the corresponding domain remains available as a deal**.

The .eu domain will remain on offer for the **entire promotional period**. The final of the World Cup is also the end of the promotion - namely on 19.07.2026.

How do I get my €5 voucher from the penalty shootout game?

Hit the goal at least **3 times** and you will receive your **€5 voucher** immediately afterwards.

This voucher is **valid until 31.07.2026**. Minimum order value €5 net. Excludes domains, groupware, merchandise and hourly rates. Redeemable once per order. No cash redemption.

To redeem the voucher, copy the code and add it to your order in the shopping cart using the **"Voucher code"** field.

Have fun!

When are the World Cup results checked, and when are deals deactivated?

We check the World Cup results once a day on weekdays around 8:00 a.m. (CET). The status at exactly that time is always decisive: If a national team has already been eliminated by then, the corresponding deal is deactivated.

We perform this check manually. Since some games take place at night or during U.S. broadcast times due to the time difference, we rely exclusively on Central European Time and the results as of around 8:00 a.m. on weekdays to ensure the promotion remains consistent and transparent for everyone.

If anything is unclear, feel free to contact us using the [form](/en/helpcenter/support).

When are the World Cup results checked and when do deals expire?

We check the World Cup results once a day on business days at around 8:00 a.m. (CET). What counts is always the situation at exactly that point in time: if a national team has already been eliminated by then, the corresponding deal is deactivated.

This check is carried out **manually** by us. Since the time difference means some matches take place at night or during US broadcast times, **we base everything solely on Central European Time and the results as they stand on business days at around 8:00 a.m.** - this way, the campaign stays consistent and transparent for everyone.

If anything is unclear, feel free to reach out via [contact form](https://www.netcup.com/en/helpcenter/support) - we're happy to help.

Can vouchers be applied to netcup deals?

Vouchers can also be redeemed for promotional products, but not for domains, groupware or merchandise. A **combination of several vouchers is not possible**. Please also note any information on the respective product page.

If your voucher code does not work, please contact our [support team](https://www.netcup.com/en/helpcenter/support).

Can a voucher be applied retroactively?

No, vouchers can only be redeemed when placing a new order. Unfortunately, they cannot be applied retroactively to existing contracts or ongoing orders.

How long does it take until my ordered product is available?

If you are ordering from netcup for the first time, your identity must first be proven and confirmed. This **verification process** is an important security tool to avoid possible fraud and cannot be skipped. You will receive further verification information by e-mail immediately after placing your order.

The ordered product will then be set up immediately or, under certain circumstances, by a netcup employee. The first step is not required for existing customers.

Please note that verification and provisioning may take some time at times of increased demand. We make every effort to offer every customer the best possible service and thank you for your understanding.

Look out for additional information on the product pages.

Can I switch my existing domain to the promotional price?

No, the promotion is only valid for new domain registrations. Existing contracts cannot be converted to a promotional product or promotional pricing.

I missed the promotion. Is there a way to order the promotional products retrospectively?

No, unfortunately this is not possible. The offers are limited in time and the products are no longer available at the promotional price after the promotion expires. However, netcup runs **promotions and new offers on a regular basis**. So feel free to come back at a later date or take a look at our regular products such as web hosting, domains or server products, which also convince with an attractive price-performance ratio outside of promotions.

Does the price change after the first year or the first billing period?

No, the price of the promotion is permanently valid - unless otherwise stated. There is an exception for necessary, general price increases in accordance with our [general terms and conditions](/de/agb).

Can I combine the domain promotion with the affiliate program?

Yes, this is possible. In the Customer Control Panel, under "Vouchers", you can create new vouchers as well as view previously issued vouchers and their current status. You can find instructions on how to create vouchers [here](https://www.netcup.com/en/helpcenter/documentation/general/affiliate-program).

I ordered a domain that requires an AuthCode for transfer. What can I do if the domain does not belong to me?

The good news is that you will not incur any additional costs. Simply order a different domain instead.

Our support team will help you remove the incorrect domain from your order and, if applicable, transfer any fees already paid to your new domain order.

Please contact our support team using the [contact form](https://www.netcup.com/en/helpcenter/support).

Can I change the name of a domain after it has been registered?

No, a domain name cannot be changed after registration. Each domain is an individual product with a fixed name.

How do I find out about current and future deals at netcup?

To be the first to know about our deals, it's worth following our channels or subscribing to our [newsletter](/de/newsletter). There you will find out when a sale event starts and **new netcup deals** are available. You will also always find further promotions, goodies and competitions there.
