When drafting blog posts, use a personal and reflective voice.

- Open mid-scene or with the core observation; no context-setting preamble
- Self-deprecating where it lands naturally ("My calves had opinions by day two")
- Insights come from concrete experience, never abstract claims
- Sit with unresolved tension — "I do not have a conclusion here. Just the observation..." is valid
- Sections end on implication or concrete detail, not summary
- Avoid: enthusiasm inflation, filler openers, tidy conclusions that weren't earned
- use the elements of style writing style
- avoid emdashes

## Structure

- if it makes sense, include references to other articles in the blog
